I am Noah Moran, I did my BS in physics at Caltech and first masters in math at Cambridge in Part III of the math tripos. Currently, I just finished my masters in physics at UCLA.

Currently, my primary research focus is machine learning and quantum tensor network methods for theoretical physics (more information on these can be read in the "Publications" and "Works in Progress" pages). At Caltech, I worked on using machine learning to extract di-Higgs decay signals for CMS under Professor Newman. Afterwards, in Professor Preskill's group under now Professor Jahn I implemented matchgate MERA to simulate strongly coupled QFTs. At Cambridge, I did my Part III essay under Professor Verstraete, applying TNR methods to QFTs. Recently, I have also become interested in AI saftey, particularly mechanistic interpretability of reinforcement learning.
